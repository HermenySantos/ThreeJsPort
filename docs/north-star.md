# Portfolio north star

Locked with Gildo on 15 September 2026. This direction governs future design,
copy, assets, and project selection for hermenegildosantos.com. It takes priority
over earlier visual concepts, including the command-rig hero specification.

## Purpose

Build a shop window that sells before a conversation. Within ten seconds, a
recruiter or hiring manager weighing money and risk should understand what Gildo
ships, what he owns, and why the work merits serious consideration at the top of
the market. The rest of the page must earn that first impression with proof.

## Story

Lead with one primary shipped-work story and two supporting shipped-work stories.
Choose them for the strength of the evidence and their relevance to the intended
buyer. Explain the problem, the stakes, Gildo's specific responsibility, the hard
decisions, and the outcome. Give collaborators accurate credit.

The site should communicate that Gildo can be trusted with a difficult week and
carry work through to delivery. A project gallery, stack list, or embellished CV
cannot substitute for that story.

## Craft

Premium presentation is a requirement: intentional typography, composition,
spacing, imagery, and restrained motion. Quiet, specific confidence should guide
every frame and line. Existing 3D scenes and interactions must earn their place
by making the work or its value clearer.

The core message and first proof must be readable quickly on mobile as well as
desktop, without waiting for animation or exploring an interaction.

## Evidence and discretion

Make the proof easy to understand. Prefer a concrete, defensible outcome and a
clear account of ownership over superlatives. Use verified metrics when available;
otherwise describe an observable result without inventing numbers or implying
unproven commercial impact.

Stay Dorier-safe throughout: respect client confidentiality, protect internal
details, and represent teammates fairly. Use public or cleared material, or
carefully abstracted examples that cannot expose protected information. Label
reconstructions and illustrative demos honestly. Claim end-to-end ownership only
where it is accurate.

## Decision filter

For every proposed look, line of copy, or asset, state which of these it serves:

- Immediate understanding of what Gildo delivers and for whom.
- Credible evidence of ownership, judgment, and shipped outcomes.
- Confidence in craft, reliability, and discretion.
- An easy next step for a visitor already persuaded by the proof.

Cut elements that serve none of these. Visual ambition should strengthen the
evidence; every claim must remain supportable.

## Success

The intended result is better-qualified inbound, conversations that begin with
confidence in Gildo's value, and stronger compensation discussions. The same
evidence should support a side business or product story without exaggeration.
These are objectives, not promises or claims to publish as achieved results.

LinkedIn and X remain mostly quiet. The site carries the selling; an ongoing
social publishing programme is outside this direction.
